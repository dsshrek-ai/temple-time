Temple Time

Instructions

Getting Started

  • Go to Temples and tap + Add Temple for each temple you visit or hope to visit.
  • Go to People and add the family and friends you attend with.
  • Then start logging visits — the Dashboard fills in as you go.

Logging a Visit

  • Tap Quick Log, pick the temple and date, and save. That's all that's required.
  • Open Add More Details to record who you went with, purpose, work performed, and journal notes.
  • On a past visit, Duplicate as New Visit copies it so a repeat trip takes seconds.

Planning a Visit

  • Tap Plan a Visit to put a future trip on your list.
  • Check Appointment Scheduled once you've booked it with the temple — unbooked plans show a red warning.
  • Use Navigate for directions and Add to Google Calendar to put it on your calendar.
  • After you go, tap Log This Visit on the plan to turn it into a visit.

Photos

  • Tap + Add Photo on a temple or visit. Photos from your phone are resized automatically.
  • Open a photo to add a caption, tag people, or set it as the Temple Primary Photo or Visit Cover Photo.

People

  • Open a person and tap Add Photo to give them a picture. Tap Change Photo to replace it.
  • Each person's page shows how many visits and temples you've shared.
  • To hide someone without losing their history, tap Edit Person and uncheck Active.

Memory Book & Statistics

  • Export Memory Book on a temple or person creates a PDF of your visits and photos.
  • Statistics shows streaks, most-visited temples, and who you attend with most.

Sharing Your Temple List

  • On Temples, tap Share My List, then Generate Share Code. You can choose to include your upcoming plans.
  • Give the code to another Temple Time user. They enter it under Import from a Share Code.
  • Codes work once and expire after 48 hours. Your visits and journal stay private.
